About Analytico LLC

Analytico LLC is a small, independent design shop. We create original artwork for the things people love, from fishing and aviation to faith, family and favorite holidays, and put it on products that are printed to order and shipped straight to you.

The shop was started by Bill Young, Ph.D., an engineer and analytics professor who enjoys turning ideas into designs. That same analytics background shapes how we run the shop: we test, measure and keep improving so the right design finds the right customer.

Our products are printed and shipped by our production partner, Printful, and sold through Etsy, so every purchase comes with Etsy's buyer protection.
