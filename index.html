Privacy Policy — Analytico Listing Poster

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy covers the "Analytico Listing Poster" application (the "App"), a personal automation tool operated by Analytico LLC. The App connects to Pinterest's API to publish product images from the Analytico LLC Etsy shop to Analytico LLC's own Pinterest account.

Who this applies to

This App is used solely by Analytico LLC for its own business purposes. It is not distributed to, or used by, the general public or any third party. There are no end users of this App other than its owner.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information from users. It does not have a login system, does not accept input from visitors, and does not track, profile, or identify any individual.

The only data the App handles is:

  • Product images and listing information that Analytico LLC already owns and has published publicly on its own Etsy shop.
  • Pinterest API access credentials (OAuth tokens) belonging to Analytico LLC's own Pinterest Business account, used solely to authenticate the App's own automated posts.

Payments

This App does not process payments, accept orders, or handle any payment or financial information. Analytico LLC's Etsy shop, where products are sold, uses Etsy's own payment processing system; no payment data passes through or is stored by this App.

Data sharing

This App does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties beyond the Pinterest API itself, which is used only to publish content that is already public on Analytico LLC's Etsy shop.

Data retention

The App retains only: (1) Pinterest OAuth tokens necessary for continued API access, and (2) a short-lived internal queue of pending posts, which is deleted once each post is published.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App's functionality changes. Since the App has no external users, no notice mechanism is required, but any updates will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Analytico LLC via the contact information listed on its Etsy shop page.
