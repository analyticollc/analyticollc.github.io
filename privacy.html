Privacy Policy

Analytico LLC · Last updated October 28, 2026

Analytico LLC sells print-on-demand products through our Etsy shop. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and how to reach us.

Information We Collect

When you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy shares the details needed to fill your order, such as your name, shipping address and what you ordered. We use that information only to make and ship your order and to answer your questions. Payments are handled by Etsy; we never see your card details.

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising trackers.

Who We Share It With

Orders are printed and shipped by our production partner, Printful, which receives the shipping details needed to deliver your order. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Our Tools and Connected Services

We use our own internal software to manage our shop listings. It connects to our own accounts on Etsy and Pinterest through their official APIs to update our product listings and to post Pins for our own products to our own Pinterest boards. These tools are used only by Analytico LLC. They do not access other people's Pinterest or Etsy accounts and do not collect data from visitors or customers. Any account tokens are stored privately on our own computers.

We may use AI services to help write product descriptions and images for our own listings. No customer information is sent to these services.

How Long We Keep It

We keep order information only as long as Etsy, tax and business record rules require.

Your Choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Etsy's own privacy policy also applies to purchases made on Etsy.
